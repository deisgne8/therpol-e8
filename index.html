01 · The problem

Without Therpol,it snaps.

Brittle plastic gives little warning: fine crazes, then fracture, with almost no stretch. It is one reason so much recycled plastic is downcycled.

02 · The mix

Same recycled base.Add Therpol.

A patented, natural-rubber-based additive, compounded into the same recycled plastic. Same bar, same test.

03 · With Therpol

It yieldsinstead of breaking.

Past its peak load the bar gives way gradually: a neck forms in the middle, and the material starts to draw.

03 · With Therpol

With Therpol,it stretches.

The neck travels along the bar and the plastic keeps drawing, long after the untreated bar broke. The live site plots Therpol's validated figures here.

04 · The difference

Same base.One additive.

The area under each curve is the energy the material absorbs before it fails: its toughness. Tougher recycled plastic can go back into products that ask more of it.
